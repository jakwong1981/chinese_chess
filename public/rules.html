Game Overview 遊戲概述

Chinese chess, or Xiangqi (象棋), is a two-player strategy game played on a 9×10 board divided by a river in the middle. Each player controls 16 pieces: one General (King), two Advisors, two Elephants, two Horses, two Chariots (Rooks), two Cannons, and five Soldiers (Pawns). The goal is to checkmate the opponent's General. 中國象棋是兩人對弈的策略遊戲，棋盤為 9×10 格，中間以「楚河漢界」分隔。每方有 16 枚棋子：一個帥（將）、兩個仕（士）、兩個相（象）、兩個馬、兩個車、兩個炮、五個兵（卒）。目標是將死對方的帥（將）。

The Board 棋盤

  • 9 files (columns) × 10 ranks (rows) — pieces are placed on intersections, not squares. 9 路 × 10 行 — 棋子放在交叉點上，而非格子內。
  • The River (楚河漢界) — divides the board between ranks 4 and 5. Elephants cannot cross; Soldiers gain sideways movement after crossing. 楚河漢界 — 將棋盤分為第 4 行與第 5 行之間。相（象）不能過河；兵（卒）過河後可以橫走。
  • The Palace (九宮) — a 3×3 area in the center of each side's back rank. The General and Advisors must stay inside. 九宮 — 每方底線中央的 3×3 區域。帥（將）和仕（士）必須在九宮內活動。
Piece Placement 棋子擺放

Red pieces start on ranks 0–4 (bottom), Black on ranks 5–9 (top). The board is symmetrical — both sides have identical piece arrangements. 紅方棋子從第 0–4 行（底部）開始，黑方從第 5–9 行（頂部）開始。棋盤是對稱的——雙方有相同的棋子排列。

Pieces & Movement 棋子與走法

帥 將
General (King) 帥 / 將
Moves one step orthogonally (up/down/left/right). Must stay in the palace. Cannot face the opponent's General directly (flying general rule). 每次走一步，只能直走（上下左右）。必須在九宮內。不能與對方帥（將）直接面對面（飛將規則）。
仕 士
Advisor 仕 / 士
Moves one step diagonally. Must stay in the palace. Protects the General. 每次斜走一步。必須在九宮內。保護帥（將）。
相 象
Elephant 相 / 象
Moves exactly two steps diagonally (like a blocked bishop). Cannot cross the river. Blocked if the "eye" (intermediate point) is occupied. 每次斜走兩步（像被阻擋的主教）。不能過河。如果「象眼」（中間點）被佔據則被阻擋。
傌 馬
Horse 傌 / 馬
Moves in an "L" shape: one step orthogonally, then one step diagonally outward. Can be blocked at the orthogonal step ("horse leg"). 走「日」字形：先直走一步，再斜走一步。如果直走的一步被阻擋（「蹩馬腿」），則不能走。
俥 車
Chariot (Rook) 俥 / 車
Moves any number of steps orthogonally (like a chess rook). The most powerful piece. 可以直走任意步數（像國際象棋的車）。是最強的棋子。
炮 砲
Cannon 炮 / 砲
Moves like a Chariot (orthogonally), but captures by jumping over exactly one piece (the "screen"). 移動方式像車（直走），但吃子時必須跳過恰好一個棋子（「炮架」）。
兵 卒
Soldier (Pawn) 兵 / 卒
Moves one step forward. After crossing the river, can also move one step sideways. Never moves backward. Promotes to a more mobile piece upon reaching the enemy back rank. 每次向前走一步。過河後也可以橫走一步。永遠不能後退。到達對方底線後成為更靈活的棋子。

Special Rules 特殊規則

Flying General (飛將) 飛將

The two Generals cannot face each other directly on the same file with no pieces between them. A move that would create this situation is illegal. 兩個帥（將）不能在同一條直線上直接面對面，中間沒有任何棋子。會造成這種情況的走法是非法的。

Horse Leg Block (蹩馬腿) 蹩馬腿

If the orthogonal step of a Horse's move is blocked by another piece, the Horse cannot complete its "L" movement in that direction. 如果馬的直走一步被其他棋子阻擋，馬就不能向該方向完成「日」字形的走法。

Elephant Eye Block (塞象眼) 塞象眼

If the intermediate diagonal point (the "eye") of an Elephant's two-step diagonal move is occupied, the Elephant is blocked and cannot move there. 如果相（象）斜走兩步的中間對角點（「象眼」）被佔據，相（象）就被阻擋，不能走到那裡。

Cannon Screen (炮架) 炮架

A Cannon captures by jumping over exactly one piece (any color). That piece is the "screen". Without a screen, the Cannon cannot capture. 炮吃子時必須跳過恰好一個棋子（任何顏色）。那個棋子就是「炮架」。沒有炮架，炮不能吃子。

How to Win 如何獲勝

  • Checkmate (將死) — Attack the opponent's General so it cannot escape capture. 將死 — 攻擊對方的帥（將），使其無法逃脫。
  • Stalemate (困斃) — In Xiangqi, if a player has no legal moves, they lose (unlike international chess where it's a draw). 困斃 — 在象棋中，如果一方無棋可走，該方輸（不像國際象棋是和棋）。
  • Resignation (認輸) — A player may resign at any time. 認輸 — 玩家可以隨時認輸。
  • Timeout (超時) — If a player's clock runs out, they lose. 超時 — 如果玩家的時間用完，則輸掉比賽。
Important Note 重要提示

Perpetual check and perpetual chase are generally not allowed in competitive Xiangqi. The player initiating the repetition must vary their moves. 在競技象棋中，通常不允許長將和長捉。發起重複走法的玩家必須改變走法。

Tips for Beginners 初學者提示

  • Control the center — Pieces are more active in the middle of the board. 控制中心 — 棋子在棋盤中央更活躍。
  • Develop your pieces quickly — Don't move the same piece multiple times in the opening. 快速出子 — 開局時不要多次移動同一個棋子。
  • Protect your General — Keep Advisors and Elephants nearby for defense. 保護帥（將） — 讓仕（士）和相（象）在附近防守。
  • Use Cannons wisely — They need a screen to capture, so position them behind other pieces. 善用炮 — 炮需要炮架才能吃子，所以將它們放在其他棋子後面。
  • Cross the river with Soldiers — They become much stronger after crossing. 兵（卒）過河 — 過河後變得更強。
  • Watch for forks — Attack two pieces at once with Chariots or Cannons. 注意捉雙 — 用車或炮同時攻擊兩個棋子。
  • Trade wisely — Don't trade a valuable piece for a less valuable one unless there's a clear advantage. 明智交換 — 除非有明顯優勢，否則不要用高價值棋子換低價值棋子。
  • Learn basic openings — Central Cannon, Screen Horse, and Same-Direction Cannons are popular starts. 學習基本開局 — 中炮、屏風馬、順手炮是流行的開局。
